1. The scenography shouldn’t feel divided—visitors should step into the installation as part of the total scenography. Otherwise, it might seem like they’re leaving the Summit and entering another Kunsthal room.

2. The proposal includes a non-rectangular space (note the angled entrance) measuring approximately 4.2 x 5.5 meters, making it generously sized.

4. This layout ensures a manageable flow from the entrance to the beanbags, making navigation intuitive.

5. It ensures sufficient darkness for the projection regardless of other light sources in the room.

6. The immersive experience is focused, avoiding the extended space becoming too cinema-like and ensuring the projection remains impactful and legible between images, especially if one is to follow the process from speech2image.

## A25-S32: Performance proposal

*The Algorithmic Oracle *
In this performance the artist steps into the role of an AI oracle,
embodying a hybrid of human and machinic consciousness. They are dressed
ritually - perhaps something like the Bene Gesserit priestesses of the DUNE
novels, or more comically, an Easyjet air hostess. With a set of specially
designed tarot cards that blend the mystical symbolism of The Major Arcana with
futuristic AI imagery, the artist offers a provocative reading inspired by
a question arrived at by the whole table, or just one person. Combining
elements of performance art, participatory installation, and speculative
fiction, the performance explores the intersections of human intuition,
artificial intelligence, and the desire for insight into our uncertain
futures.
