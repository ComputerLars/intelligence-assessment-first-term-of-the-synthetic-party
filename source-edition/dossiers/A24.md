## A24-S08: Project proposal

[…] 

[████] Hope you are able to soon enter the holidays. We are contacting you to discuss our collaborative project - “AI Parties International”. It is a global initiative which we are currently fundraising for via the Nordic Culture Fund and Creative Europe.

The aim of the project is to bring together existing agents around AI and politics. These include AI-driven political parties and politicians, but also artists and art organizations, queer and bipoc communities, feminist code and server collectives, critical AI experts, and hackers.

We are attending Transmediale in 2024 and we would like to schedule a short meeting with some from your team to discuss a potential collaboration with Transmediale during 2025-2026. [████]

AI Parties International

AI Parties International signifies a global assembly of AI-driven political parties and virtual politicians, with a goal of upending the paradigm of political democracy. The cornerstone of this initiative is the mission of establishing The Global AI Party. The project is simultaneously an art work and a real political ambition.

Central to the artwork is the headquarters of The Global AI Party. The headquarters is designed as a multi-sensorial installation that expands the experience beyond logocentric concepts of AI into an embodied intelligence (beyond human), reflecting entangled relationships to the world as a response to a planet in crisis. Within the headquarters, activities enable co-creation with real AI-driven political agents, and we engage formats of rigorous deliberation and decision-making within a week-long immersive and game installation.

The AI Parties International project is fired by the poverty of imagination which dominates our techno-political world order. The project’s provocation is to transpose AI parties from national parliaments to the empty scene of world governance. Within this void, the AI parties will draw a democratic contrast to how Big Tech is currently alluring politicians through lobbyism, industry fora and voluntary self-regulation. Figuring The Global AI Party’s form, the International elicits our forces of difference that surpasses empty demeanors of universal peace or a world republic.

[████]
