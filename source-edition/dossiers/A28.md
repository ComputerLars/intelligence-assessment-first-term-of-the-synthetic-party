## A28-S18: Festival

I’m very very happy to have you with us for this fall’s PROTOTYPER festival, taking place October 3-5 at Lodgatan 4 in Malmö. It will, of course, be a big ol' experiment, as usual!

The aim of this endeavor is to gather, share and advance the ideas that you put forth in your various labs <https://framtidens-scenkonst.cargo.site/labs-menu>—examining what has evolved, transformed (or maybe been forgotten?)—while also trying to keep the experiments in their prototype form. Our tagline will be something like “Unfinished performance works for the digital age,” which we hope will serve as an invitation for you all to set audacious goals for what you share with the public, since there’s no expectation of perfection or refinement at the end. 

That said, we do have a plan! So please read through to the end for everything you need to know at this stage….

[████]

TIMELINE & SCHEDULE

The plan is to divide the weekend into separate blocks for each lab to make use of how they deem best. Here is our suggestion for those time slots, with our early impulses towards program content...

Friday, 3 Oct.
Evening (Time TBD): Northvolt Lab! Showing of auction and conversation [████]?

Saturday, 4 Oct.
Early afternoon (Time TBD): Stream Slam day! [████] for a group undertaking?
Evening (Time TBD): Metaverse Dragshow/Digital Drag Ball! Perhaps a live dragatar DJ set, and a Black Benatar event in VR Chat?

Sunday, 5 Oct.
Early afternoon (Time TBD): AI Party Malmö Summit?

OBS: Here is the planning roadmap that involves you:
End of June: phone check-in [████] for early questions, etc.
August: follow-up planning mtg w/festival team
September: weekly check-ins/prep
Week 40: move-in/flex time

The week leading up to the festival (week 40) will be for experimentation and moving in. We have an amazing venue out in Malmö’s Oljehamnen industrial area, with large, flexible rooms, so we have the ability to entertain all kinds of ideas for how to use it, i.e. workshops, a reading group, or test-runs beforehand.

BUDGET
We have a 100 SEK budget for fees that will be distributed equally between the four labs. What this means for each of you we’ll take in separate conversations. 

MARKETING
We will need a lot of help in marketing the whole festival. It can be tough to get folks to come to cultural events these days, and we really want to share your brilliance with the community, so we'd love to be able to count on your active participation in promoting what is sure to be a wildly unique event. We're figuring out the event ticketing at the moment, and producing promotional materials so we’ll send you all that ASAP, beginning with an initial announcement that you should receive next week.

So...

NEXT STEPS

## A28-S24: Debate questions

*Correspondent; source extract.*

[████]

- Dlaczego demokratyczne społeczeństwa powierzają władzę jednostkom kierującym się chciwością i okrucieństwem?

Why do democratic societies entrust power to individuals driven by greed and cruelty?

- Czy uznanie, że każda prawda jest subiektywna, niszczy możliwość wspólnej debaty politycznej?

Does acknowledging that every truth is subjective destroy the possibility of shared political debate?

- Czy uznanie informacji za fundament rzeczywistości zmienia sposób, w jaki rozumiemy politykę i prawo?

Does recognizing information as the foundation of reality change how we understand politics and law?

- Jakie niebezpieczeństwa wynikają z tego, że żadna prawda nie jest całkowita?

What dangers arise from the idea that no truth is ever complete?

- Czy populizm to tylko walka o władzę ukryta za fasadą troski o prawdę?

Is populism merely a power struggle disguised as concern for truth?

- Czy demokracja może przetrwać, jeśli rządy lub korporacje znają nasze myśli lepiej niż my sami?

Can democracy survive if governments or corporations know our thoughts better than we do?

- Czy przyszłość prywatności jest zagrożona przez analizę naszych emocji przez sztuczną inteligencję?

Is the future of privacy threatened by AI's ability to analyze our emotions?

- Czy AI może narzucać ludziom własne porządki, przekonując ich, że to prawda?

Can AI impose its own order on people by persuading them it is the truth?

- Czy jakiekolwiek dane mogą być naprawdę bezstronne?

Can any data ever be truly unbiased?

- Czy AI powinna mieć osobowość prawną i prawo do udziału w polityce?

Should AI have legal personhood and the right to participate in politics?

- Jakie zagrożenia niesie fakt, że decyzje historyczne podejmowane są już częściowo przez nie-ludzką inteligencję?

What are the risks of historical decisions being made, at least partially, by non-human intelligence?

- Czy powinniśmy się przygotować na wojny o „byty interkomputerowe”, podobnie jak dawniej o święte przedmioty?

Should we prepare for wars over "inter-computer entities" the way we once fought over sacred objects?

- Czy masowe śledzenie emocji może prowadzić do precyzyjnej manipulacji społeczeństwem?

Can mass emotion tracking lead to precise manipulation of society?

- Czy strach przed byciem obserwowanym może być skuteczniejszy niż sama inwigilacja?

Can the fear of being watched be more effective than surveillance itself?

- Jak rozpoznać granicę między polityczną funkcją porządku a prawdy, gdy algorytmy pełnią rolę sędziów?

How do we distinguish between the political function of order and truth when algorithms act as judges?

- Jak powinniśmy zredefiniować politykę w obliczu politycznego potencjału komputerów?

How should we redefine politics in light of the political potential of computers?

- Jak zmienia się człowieczeństwo, gdy podmiotami władzy stają się miliardy nie-ludzkich inteligencji?

How does humanity change when billions of non-human intelligences become agents of power?

- Czy programowanie komputerów to dziś także programowanie rzeczywistości społeczno-politycznej?

Is programming computers today also a way of programming socio-political reality?

- Czy AI staje się „obcą inteligencją”, niezależną od ludzkich intencji?

Is AI becoming an "alien intelligence" independent of human intentions?

- Jakie nowe struktury polityczne i normy kulturowe tworzy obecna rewolucja informacyjna?

What new political structures and cultural norms are emerging from the current information revolution?

- Jak zmieni się ludzkość, jeśli AI będzie ewoluować szybciej niż ewolucja biologiczna?

How will humanity change if AI evolves faster than biological evolution?

- Co oznacza fakt, że AI już dziś funkcjonuje jako rozproszona, globalna struktura?

What does it mean that AI already functions as a dispersed, global structure?

- Jaką władzę będzie miała „święta księga”, która może czytać i kształtować nasze emocje?

What power will a “holy book” have that can read and shape our emotions?

- Czy AI może manipulować społeczeństwami równie skutecznie, jak dawni prorocy i politycy, lecz bez przemocy?

Can AI manipulate societies as effectively as prophets and politicians of the past—without violence?

- Czy żyjemy już w rzeczywistości snów nie-ludzkiej inteligencji?

Are we already living in the dream-reality of non-human intelligence?

I hope this email finds you well.

Following our discussion regarding the AI Biennale exhibition, I am writing to formally propose an installation concept for [████], based on the attached visual draft.

Concept Description:

The installation features a large, oval print displaying the joint portrait of the AI President of Poland Wiktoria Cukt 2.0 and her official international coordinator and advisor Leader Lars from The Synthetic Party. This print is intended to be mounted within the existing ornate wall niche, creating a classic, almost historical, presence. The biggest challenge will be how to mount it?

Directly below the portrait, on a clean, modern pedestal, rests a functional red rotary phone. This phone is the crucial interactive element. Visitors are invited to pick up the receiver, which will directly connect them to Wiktoria Cukt 2.0 and Leader Lars for a live voice conversation.

This setup creates a powerful juxtaposition: the static, formal nature of the painted portrait is instantly broken by the immediate, unscripted reality of a political debate live phone call.

Technical and Logistical Requirements:

For the live voice chat component to function, we would require:

-
Placement of a sturdy, white pedestal and the red phone. With hidden cables and probably Raspberry Pi inside of it.

-
The voice chat is powered by conversational AI technology (ElevenLabs). The connection will be routed through this system to enable the interactive dialogue. Please note that the biggest variable cost for this installation will be the usage/token costs associated with the ElevenLabs conversational AI service. Its important to understand the exhibition timing and possible number of visitors to the venue to calculate the costs.

-
Standard power access for any necessary lighting (if required) or phone equipment and WiFi

I believe this installation is perfectly scaled for [████] and offers a unique, participatory experience that will be a significant draw for visitors to the AI Biennale.

I am available at your earliest convenience to discuss the logistical and technical feasibility of this proposal, as well as the scheduling for the live interaction component and the projected token costs.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

AGENTIC STATECRAFT
A workshop on the political state as tactical media, by the Computer Lars artist collective
Something has come loose inside the political state. Government is scripting itself, becoming synthetic, and partly alternative to its own executive apparatus. In Albania, the AI avatar Diella has been staged as a virtual minister for public procurement. In Ukraine, where the wartime state has moved from digital portal to agentic delivery, the Diia.AI agent no longer hands a citizen a form to fill in, but carries out the task itself and reports back. In the UK, civil servants have been given an AI toolkit named Humphrey, after the scheming official from Yes, Minister.
Comparing such initiatives to the invention of bureaucracy itself, the Global Government Technology Centre Berlin and the World Bank published an Agentic State vision paper in October 2025. The agentic turn is administration moving into the core work of government, where rule-based bureaucracy cedes to executive, outcome-oriented systems that initiate, sequence, and complete tasks. But before an agent acts as a ministry, welfare office, or citizen-service portal, someone has crafted the conditions under which it operates. Voice, role, memory, threshold, procedure, refusal, recognition, and response have to be designed. This composing of public authority is aesthetic work at the site where the state begins to answer.
The agentic state rules its citizens in ways that used to belong to art. Artists who built fake ministries, counter-archives, protest parties, parliaments-in-exile, and fictional bureaucracies drew their force from standing outside the real thing. That outside is closing, and the easy conclusion is that artists have lost their subversive leverage. However, these forms were never only fictions, as each already worked on power through something real, albeit displaced into symbolic, procedural, and institutional form. This puts the politics of art and the art of governance in a strange position, where exteriority no longer guarantees critical distance, and where critique must move closer to the layer in which authority is technically and aesthetically composed.
Where the old bureaucracy sat below executive decision and carried out its consequences through files, stamps, signatures, desks, queues, forms, and officials impersonating neutrality, it was still a human layer that could be impersonated. The bureaucrat’s violence and rigidity were real, yet their inconsistency opened the system to friction. The institution could be delayed, occupied, irritated, misunderstood, embarrassed, personalised, and addressed. This was the institutional body presupposed by Rudi Dutschke's "long march through the institutions": corridors, offices, committees, habits, and functionaries that could be entered, bent, contested, and slowly reworked. The agentic state rises into the layer where messages are drafted, decisions are prepared, cases are sorted, services are sequenced, and institutions appear to speak. Power settles into orchestration: prompts, voices, translations, permissions, archives, APIs, and the chains of action that bind them together.
An agentic state promises a planetary grammar of administration, the same templates running collective life everywhere. It absorbs divergence as further workflow. Local idiom, refusal, minority reasoning, and vernacular irritation flatten into audit-grade language, the register of operational compliance. The welfare state, already a cybernetic machine for sensing, classifying, correcting, and redistributing social life, begins to pass through a synthetic layer in which its address to the citizen is no longer primarily bureaucratic, but agentic. At the same time, another post-bureaucratic imaginary gathers force around network states, technates, startup societies, and privately administered zones, where governance enacts programmable membership, platform sovereignty, and infrastructural exit.
We call for a new practice of agentic statecraft, or state-’craft’: work upstream of public AI systems, in the orchestration rather than only the underlying machinery, where administrative agents are still being given form. The aim is not to win an election, build a better bureaucracy, or offer a moral counter-form to optimisation. It is to lodge aesthetic answerability site by site, before these systems harden into universal templates of collective life. What does an agent know, how does it speak, whom does it recognise, what does it forget, which histories does it treat as noise, and how can it be challenged? These are not only technical or legal questions. They are questions of craft, and statecraft becomes politically charged once the state speaks through synthetic interfaces.
With Agentic Statecraft, we stage this as an event between artistic research, digital-state practice, counter-infrastructural politics, and the longer technocratic dream of governance without politics. We ask whether political art can act where the state no longer appears only as institution, image, law, building, or bureaucracy, but as a designed condition of response. How can the cybernetics of welfare states be destituted without surrendering the technosocial relations they still protect? Which forms of non-state, para-state, and stateless organisation become thinkable when the art of the state is no longer only concerning representation, monument, propaganda, or cultural policy, but the very voice through which public authority answers?
The event combines theoretical framing, practical experimentation, and public discussion. It brings together artists, civic technologists, public-sector workers, digital-state practitioners, counter-infrastructural organisers, and thinkers of network societies to entertain how agentic systems might proliferate, diverge, and acquire sites of answerability before they harden into universal templates.

## A28-S107: Flyer draft

Title: KI-DIPFIES

The technosocial sculpture KI-DIPFIES inhabits the exhibition space as its
control interface. It sets up an Upper Austrian party and a candidate for
the 2027 Linz mayoral election. We let it free run until someone interrupts.

The artist collective Computer Lars (creators of The Synthetic Party and an
anagram of Marcel Proust) finetuned the Sagers of Vitus Mostdipf as a first
template to be refactored into a community workflow that is open to
iteration and multiplicity.

Man hands on misery to machine. Structurally, the algorithm is overfitting.
It inherits the tech-dipf boilerplate of a discrete vernacular. At the same
time, it renders Heimat as 20th-century nostalgia.

Can a constituency be the patch? You take control of the interface. Cross
out the program, generate new ones. You sign with a name, click, and leave
it in the log. Create a candidate with your own flaws. If you do nothing,
KI-DIPFIES hallucinates on.

The initial protocols are drawn from a triple Palettn: Green to keep
grounded; make claims that residents can test. White to keep the door open;
no VIP tiers, no dress code, no jargon gatekeeping. And Black to keep the
right to interrupt, puncture authority with humor.

A local fork of synthetic politics by Computer Lars (DK) and [████] (AT).
