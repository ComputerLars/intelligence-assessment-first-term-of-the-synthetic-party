# Edition note

**Intelligence Assessment: First Term of The Synthetic Party · Source dossiers · v.01**

The European Democracy Sword is a fictional investigating bureau whose agents assemble, examine and screen the evidence in this registry. The source records predate that fiction; their claims are material for the inquiry, not findings endorsed by the register.

## Holdings

The register contains **3,056 records in 32 dossiers**: 2,587 email records and 469 document, data and media records. Of these, 2,991 contain documentary text, code, data, images or audio, in full or in extracts; 61 preserve only brief wording, source metadata or limited visual context; 4 remain indexed without reproduced content.

These totals count registered records, including repeated witnesses, not people, events or separate files. Repeated material remains where it documents a different occurrence or context. Byte-identical images may share one file while retaining both record identifiers. The [catalogue](CATALOGUE.tsv) records the extent of each representation; the [file register](SOURCE_FILES.tsv) maps files to records. The Malmö lecture and counting methods are separate supplements.

## Redactions and reproduction

The investigating agents prepare the dossiers for disclosure. **[████]** marks redaction; **[…]** marks an omitted passage. Some witnesses also use bracketed omission notes. Names, contact details, account keys and identifying private context are removed from correspondence and conversations. Public authorship, performances and project credits remain. Redaction does not establish anonymity where other evidence identifies a person.

Retained source wording has not been rewritten into new dialogue. Disagreement, criticism, costs and failed experiments remain part of the record. Transcriptions, extracts and facsimiles are distinguished; image masks replace the stored pixels. Missing words behind earlier redactions are not reconstructed. The original files are preserved separately.

**A29-D19 and A23-D10–D12 are not reproduced.** The three SAM documents were supplied for private study; publication permission is not documented.

## Source limits

Automatic transcripts may contain recognition errors. The Malmö transcript has not received a complete listening check. Language classifications describe the earlier analysed Discord witness, before this edition's further redactions. The supplements document these limits and retain their original denominators.
