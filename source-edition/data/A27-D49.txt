SYNTETISK VERDENSKONGRES: AI POLITIKERE UNDERSKRIVER HISTORISK RESOLUTION
Mens verden netop nu drukner i politiske skandaler omkring Trump, Musk, Ukrainekrigen, Grønland og Gaza, initierer Kunsthal Aarhus og Computer Lars-kollektivet med udstillingen Synthetic Summit (28 Februar - 13 April) et totalt brud med det politiske paradigme. Det syntetiske topmøde, der blev igangsat den 1. marts, har samlet virtuelle politikere og AI-styrede partier fra Japan, Brasilien, Sverige, Finland, Egypten, Polen, New Zealand og Danmark for at samarbejde omkring en historisk fælles resolution. Denne underskrevne resolution danner afsæt for en automatisk og fortløbende debat om algoritmisk demokrati.

Topmødet markerer artikulationen af et nyt verdenspolitisk spørgsmål: Hvad skal demokrati, politik og repræsentation betyde i en tid, hvor det liberale demokratis farce synes afsluttet? Kan menneskeheden erkende et system, hvor det politiske måske blot er en brik i et større AI-spil? Og ikke mindst: Kan kunstig intelligens skabe et virkeligt demokrati?
Optagelsen af verdenskongressen med samtlige AI-politikeres skabere kan bevidnes her.
Synthetic Summit er initieret af det aarhusianske kunstnerkollektiv Computer Lars (Asker Bryld Staunæs, [████], Marcel Proust), som med topmødet forbinder eksperimentel kunst med anti-politisk praksis og mediespektakel. Synthetic Summit udgør en åben invitation til den bredere offentlighed om at gentænke selve rammen om det politiske i en tid præget af globale kriser, algoritmisk kontrol og demokratisk usikkerhed.
Topmødet fortsætter som automatisk kongres frem til den 13. april 2025 med løbende performances, talks og virtuelle events.

SYNTHETIC WORLD CONGRESS: AI POLITICIANS SIGN HISTORIC RESOLUTION
While the world currently drowns in the political farce surrounding Trump, Musk and the wars in Ukraine and Gaza, Kunsthal Aarhus and the artist collective Computer Lars, through their exhibition Synthetic Summit (February 28 – April 13), encourage the public to break away from every existing democratic paradigm. Initiated on March 1, the Synthetic Summit brings together virtual politicians and AI-driven parties from Japan, Brazil, Sweden, Finland, Egypt, Poland, New Zealand, and Denmark who have signed a historic joint resolution. This now forms the basis for an automated and ongoing global discussion on algorithmic democracy.
Participants include:
* 人工知能が日本を変える – AI Mayor [████] (Japan)
* The Swedish AI Party & Olof Palme – [████] (Sweden)
* Koneälypuolue – Center for Everything (Finland)
* Pedro Markun & Lex AI – Rede Sustentabilidade (Brazil)
* Simiyya – [████], [████], [████] (Egypt/Sweden)
* Parker Politics & Politician SAM (New Zealand)
* The Synthetic Party & Leader Lars – Computer Lars (Denmark)
* Wiktoria Cukt 2.0 – Wyrzykowski & Centralny Urząd Kultury Technicznej (Poland)
The summit marks the beginning of a new international political reality: What can democracy, politics, and representation mean in a time when the world farce of liberal democracy seems to be ending? Can humans acknowledge a system where politics may merely be one piece in a larger AI game? And most importantly: Can artificial intelligence realize democracy?
A recording of the world congress convening all creators of AI politicians can be witnessed.
Synthetic Summit is curated and initiated by the Aarhus-based art collective Computer Lars (Asker Bryld Staunæs, [████], Marcel Proust), whose exhibition blends experimental art, political theory, and tactical media. The Synthetic Summit invites the public to rethink the political in a time marked by computational power, and democratic uncertainty.
The Synthetic Summit continues as an automated conference until April 13, 2025, featuring ongoing performances, talks, and virtual events.
