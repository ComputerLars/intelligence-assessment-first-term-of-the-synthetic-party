One of the key lessons I learned with The Synthetic Party is that the classical idea of a party representing the political opinions of its constituency has been troubled in the digital age, and was perhaps never true. Most parties since the 1980’s infer opinions based on polls. [████]The Synthetic Party also just had the opinions that Leader Lars replied at Discord. This means that the political element becomes much more affective and emotional: a contingent generation of what the constituency asks for on a day-to-day basis. This is also akin to what [████] already did with Wiktoria Cukt, where she held all the political views people wrote on their web portal.
