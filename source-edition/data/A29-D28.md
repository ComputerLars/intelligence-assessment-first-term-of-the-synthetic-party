### M00526 | Operator

[████]

I came to imagine the possibility of an international, because The Synthetic Party quite paradoxically became a sort of "synthetic international" by itself. I had spent a lot of time localizing and framing the project within the cultural codes of Danish politics, in a counter-move to the homogenizing and universalizing tendencies of AI and politics. However, firstly due to a press release by the Agence France Presse and later through an article by Vice that went viral during the elections, the impact of The Synthetic Party became highly international. People from all over the world had Discord-conversations with the virtual politician chatbot, even though it could only reply in Danish, and there were news media articles from major outlets from all countries in the world. 

So it became a kind of "global media novel" where each country had its own story about the AI party ...

My conceptual thoughts with The Synthetic Party as a mirror and molder has very much been developed through Joseph Beuys' concept of "social sculpture", which he originally designated to describe mock political parties and organizations such as The German Student Party, Organization of Non-Voters, etc.

### M00528 | Operator

So, to also be more concrete in terms of your proposals 🙂

1) I believe the International should use our imaginative competencies as artists to achieve a high degree of reality and importance. I think it would be cool if we actually reached some decision-making that could have impact.

2)  I love the techniques of worldbuilding and performance. In my practice, I'm mostly doing that discursively but with delegation of tasks between us I think we could make a quite expansive imaginary for political AI that goes beyond the bureaucratic horizon of algorithmic governmentality.

3) I would love for the artist-driven AI parties to take charge of coordinating and facilitating the meeting, but that we also include other actors - non-artists and BIPOC - to make the cosmo-technical gesture  strong.

4) I'm artistically better equipped at "the rigorous discursive forms (discussions, publications, political interventions etc.)" versus the "immersive and game performances" AND I would find it really interesting to play and experiment with the immersive-gamified aspects WITHIN discursive forms. I also love the technical side of AI, and to create techno-social public spaces as a conflictual (!) political domain.

5) I think we have some good opportunities with Kunsthal Aarhus and Transmediale to get started in  2025. But I would also love for us to "prepare the ground" through media interventions and possibly by setting up some online AI agency beforehand. I'm reading lots of technical papers on AI and democracy, which also have some great ideas - see eg https://www.anthropic.com/index/collective-constitutional-ai-aligning-a-language-model-with-public-input or https://github.com/openai/democratic-inputs

6) Playfulness is the most important thing to establish a fruitful collaboration, I think. It would be great if we can play around together with ideas, narratives and visions together in the february meetings, and then afterwards figure out who's in charge of what.
