A27-D41 | Rally programme

AI Authority of Art
Leader Lars & Wiktoria Cukt 2.0

PATIO, Academy of Fine Arts in Gdańsk
November 8, 2024
Welcome to a rally at the frontiers of political virtuality: Wiktoria Cukt 2.0, revived from Poland's 2000 presidential race, meets Leader Lars of Denmark's The Synthetic Party, established for the 2022 parliamentary election. Organized by artists [████] and Asker Bryld Staunæs, this event marks a rare convergence between two decades of politically engaged public space art within the evolving future of algorithmic democracy.
Using the technological tools of their times, both Wiktoria and Lars symbolize a shift in political agency; positioning algorithmic representation as a critique of democratic politics. These two virtual candidates emerged both as electoral guerilla performance, and as provocation of societal debates about the pervasiveness of AI, computer technology and media created representation in political discourse and everyday life.
The Rally begins with a debate between Leader Lars and Wiktoria Cukt 2.0, setting the agenda for the meeting with bot-led agitation, audiovisual propaganda, and augmented deliberation.
The event continues with performance lectures of invited guests that will unpack the roots of these projects as well as critiquing and analysing the actions and demands undertaken. 
1. Wiktoria Cukt: Born from the Polish art collective Central Office of Technical Culture (CUKT) in 2000, Wiktoria was created as a virtual presidential candidate. Her slogan, "Politicians Are Obsolete," struck at the heart of representative parliamentarism. The campaign, rooted in 'Electoral Citizen Software' ('Obywatelski Software Wyborczy'), combined various conflicting opinions sent online into a collective voice, the so-called 'Will of Wictoria Cukt'. Updated for 2024, Wiktoria Cukt 2.0. returns as a bot and haunting emblem of early AI activism, broadcasting the electoral illusionment of electronic democracy.
2. Leader Lars of The Synthetic Party: Created in 2022, the chatbot Leader Lars is the figurehead of Denmark's The Synthetic Party, which uses large language models to synthesize political opinions. Based upon discourse from 200+ micro-parties that have failed to enter parliament since 1970, Lars' AI-generated program models the voice of Denmark's disenfranchised with the aim of representing the non-voting 20%. With a global media appeal and 24/7 availability on SoMe platforms like Discord, Lars goes beyond critique—actively building worldwide coalitions between diverse political systems to enable outlets for everyone deemed resource deficient within democracy.
Following these presentations will be an open discussion on the use of AI within contemporary artistic practices. Topics can be on how the use of AI images, text and video can negotiate boundaries between art, activism, and politics, and whether one can balance the hype around AI in #art when probing into it as a potential catalyst for social transformations.
This event sets the stage for an upcoming Synthetic Summit at Kunsthal Aarhus in 2025, where AI-driven political parties and virtual politicians gather from Australia, Denmark, Egypt, Finland, Japan, Poland and Sweden to outline a program of global AI-coordination.

Asker Bryld Staunæs is a practice-based PhD philosopher affiliated with Aarhus University and Kunsthal Aarhus. He works with an expanded concept of politics, oftenly at the intersection of AI, democracy, and art. Asker becomes operative through diverse extra-disciplinary collectives such as "Computer Lars", "Center for Aesthetics of AI Images", "The Synthetic Party",  "The Organ of Autonomous Sciences", etc.


[████] alias [████] PhD in art, lecturer at the Academy of Fine Arts in Gdansk, multimedia artist, performer, stage designer, vj. Author of mobile applications, interactive installations, actions in public space, director of multimedia performances and videos. Develops the theory of transformism in art. Experiments with the language of art, explores the creative potential of contemporary communication media. 
Co-founder and artistic director of the C.U.K.T. (Central Office of Technical Culture) group, which makes prescient use of the reach of the Internet for artistic and activist activities (Victoria Cukt Presidential Campaign, 2000). 
His works can be found in numerous collections including: a video work ('Beta Nassau' 1993) is in the collection of the Museum of Modern Art, New York. The video work ('Atomic Love', 2006) is in the collection of the Musée National d'Art Moderne Centre Georges Pompidou in Paris.
