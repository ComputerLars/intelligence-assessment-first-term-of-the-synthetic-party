In an essay for TechTalks [7], Herbert Roitblat, data scientist and 
author of _Algorithms Are Not Enough_, explains why rewards are not 
enough to build artificial intelligence.

Roitblat's essay is in response to a recent paper by DeepMind 
scientists, in which they claim that rewards and reinforcement learning 
are all you need to build all the components of intelligence.

In his essay, Roitblat draws lessons from decades of work on cognitive 
science and artificial intelligence to refute the claims made by 
DeepMind.
