In this way, Det Syntetiske Parti becomes a meta-reflection on what a

2) We pose “Computer Lars” as a question or a problem that have arisen from
the works of Marcel Proust. We have treated it in detail in this artistic
research dissertation:
https://computerlars.files.wordpress.com/2022/06/nyeste-computerlars-2.pdf.

The ambition is to think through all the possible subjectivities that can
arise from the name of Marcel Proust. Therefore, Computer Lars is in the
context of Det Syntetiske Parti a way to figure out who “Lars” is. Fx, in
Denmark, there are more CEO’s that are named Lars than there are female
CEO’s.

The name of “Lars” would be equal to “John” in English, or “Francois” in
French. Brought together with the Word “computer”, which means calculation,
“Lars” signifies the cultural and political power of the common white male.

So for Det Syntetiske Parti, it is pertinent to have Computer Lars as its
party secretary, as Lars in a way mirrors the AI technology as being biased
towards a specific culture of white, powerful males.

This of course reflects back interestingly on Marcel Proust who couples a
Jewish background and a homosexual identity with inherited wealth and
ressources that in many ways made the publication of “In Search of Lost
Time” possible - just as the written output of an AI is only possible
through expensive server farms that are powered by the world’s largest
companies.

So in this way - to frame it with Walter Benjamin - the anagram of Computer
Lars is a “dialectical image” or an “allegorical picture” of the basis
structure pertaining to Marcel Proust and culture as such. This problem is
taken to its extreme consequense with the establishment of Det Syntetiske
Parti.

3) Primarily, Det Syntetiske Parti’s members consist of artists from the
group Computer Lars as well as employees from the tech hub MindFuture.
However, as we are using a language model that has collected the online
archives of written Danish language, we count as members all Danish
speakers that have an internet connection. That is 99% of the population,
or 5.940.000 members.

4) We are running for Folketinget/parliament, so everyone with a Danish
