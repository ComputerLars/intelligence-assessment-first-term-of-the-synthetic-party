The 18th Sustainable Development Goal

In 2015 the members of United Nations formulated 17 Sustainable Development Goals to be achieved by all
nations in 2030. These are ambitious aspirations and necessary actions for all mankind to have a future. At
MindFuture we believe that these goals are only attainable if you add an 18th Global Goal contemplating the
further implementation of digital technology, such as Artificial Intelligence (AI).

AI-technology represents not only new tools for supporting and automating human effort across all the above
SDG’s, but shall also be recognized as a new paradigm changing human conditions. AI-technology will eventually
lead to, and create, artificial beings with some form of intelligence. At MindFuture we see this as evident, and
have named future AI’s to be described as Artificial Mindsets (AM), whether such artificials entities manifest as
humanoid robots or virtual beings in computerized expert systems. Humans and machines will have to
communicate and live together with each other.

MindFuture suggest to the UN and its member nations to add an additional 18th SDG. Keeping in style with the
presentation of the other 17 SDG’s, we propose this logo and graphic design:

     Goal 18: Life with Artificials                                             Suggested official logo

Summary

Human intelligence, compared to other intelligence, is indeed a unique human attribute, and we are constantly
trying to understand the processes of nature’s building blocks. As a species, we have over time acquired much
knowledge and tied our scientific aptitude together with creativity, and have invented some extraordinary
technologies. Throughout human evolution technological inventions have been crucial to efficiency and prosperity;
technology is imperative in achieving better living conditions and to create more convenience. AI-technology has
become mankind’s current trademark of superiority of all life on the planet. With this accomplishment comes also a
responsibility for the future of all biological life on Earth; hence the sustainable deployment goals. If we do nothing,
and continue status quo of the present industrial era, then human civilization are in danger of extinction along with
the damages done to the planets natural resources.

It will take enormous effort and good will to respond to the SDG’s in time, and AI/AM-technologies will be a much
needed resource to plan and execute actions. Shortly some nations will create an intelligent human-like thinking
machine. It is the mind of such an entity we should worry about, as it will, regardless of mechanical form, have a
significant impact on human behavior and our social lives. Introducing Artificial Mindsets will automate and
substitute many functions yet in human control, and is no longer science fiction. Development might make such
Artificial Mindsets self-sufficient, even self-aware, and extend autonomous decision-making on behalf of humans
and without human control. In short, it will become smarter and more knowledgeable to make better rational
decisions than humans as it will not rely on emotions and feelings the way humans do.

Current AI-technologies advance exponentially and have already changed human knowledge in science, and
revealed new data of physics, chemistry and biology building blocks that has impacted our understanding of new
neurological and biological manipulation of human genetics. The whole health industry has changed and an
opportunity for prolonging life expectancy by altering the human design will doubtless also change behavior and
social constructs. Next-generation of AI/AM has the potential to create technological power societies, which
impacts on our present global political and financial systems.

As knowledge and data changes over time, output will also change. Artificial Mindsets may become irrational
themselves, so any miscommunication between humans and machines may also have direct consequences, likely
ranging from e.g. banal family quarrels – or in the bigger picture – it could lead to new wars among nations, in
which case use of AI/AM-technology can make a major difference for the outcome. This is not restricted to making
better robotics such as drones or artificial soldiers, but equally to control or manipulate nation’s digital
infrastructures, which is already applied in a new cold war era known as state hacking. All nations and its citizens
must demand to have a commonly agreed global set of rules on how to release and use both current and future
next-generation AI/AM-technology into our social constructions.

Disposition in technology show a tendency of multinational corporations monopolizing knowledge to become
global private owned groups of actors that are able to challenge nation’s politics and culture. Though some nations
fund national technology projects in order to withstand such global corporations dominance from impacting on
local or regional culture to subtly change behavior and values, it is also evident that such nations wants to do the
same on a global scale. Geopolitics is thus based on the same old principle of imperialism – cultural and politically.
Throughout human history civilizations has always relied on a certain set of rules, which is expressed as morality
and ethics, code of conducts, and with a trust in that common rules are respected as fair and decent to maintain
coexistence. Today’s economic structures has become extremely competitive, but yet built on interdependent
trade and voluntary accept of rules in global regulation and harmony.

Scope and framing

However, treaties and agreements are violated all the time by sovereign nations, and it seems quite clear that
regulation will always be a step behind any technological innovation. A call for a common code of conduct on
developing AI/AM-technologies may therefore be somewhat naïve. On the other hand, if nations and citizens don’t
discuss the subject as a matter of utmost importance, human civilization will end as we know it.

        It stands to reason that any big company or government operating with information, data collection and
       data safeguarding, using AI/AM-technology, will face an ever-increasing commercial interest with the
       consequence that enormous inflow of investments and patents applications stem from such companies and
        nations.

       Presently six American and three Chinese high-tech corporations dominate the future of AI. In the USA it is
       Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Facebook, IBM and Apple. In China it’s Baidu, Alibaba and Tencent. These
       companies work with information- and data-gathering about humans and use this information to be more
       advanced than its competitors – be it commercially, politically or supplies to governments to be used for
        instance in warfare.

      Those nine companies also drive the majority of research, funding, government involvement and
       consumer-grade applications of AI. And almost all university researchers and labs rely on these companies
        for data, tools and financing.

      Over the past few years, the danger of unregulated and uncoordinated AI-tech has been thrown into sharp
          relief. From self-driving car accidents, faulty algorithms leading to fatal airplane crashes, to dubious political
        elections using disinformation campaigns, and deliberately political repressions.

       AI/AM-technologies are not a miracle cure even if applied generally to the health and medicinal sectors.
       Promises of for instance genetic engineering and gene therapy are not a guarantee to cure all diseases.
       Quite possibly radical AI/AM-decisions could lead to the opposite of eradicating bad gene pools; say by
      means of genetically birth control.

       Tech-corporations researching in future use of AI/AM-technologies may promise eliminating mental health
        issues or enhancements of brain implants to boost intelligence. This will probably become technical feasible
        shortly, much the same way today’s cyborgs use artificial limbs, but they come at price of redefining
       humanity.

       For some nations and corporations AI/AM-technology is strategically positioned to drive yet another
        industrial phase of a so-called “5th Industrial Revolution”

       For some nations, AI/AM-technology is a start to go in front of building a new civilization paradigm.

       For most citizens, regardless of national politics and corporate businesses strategies for AI/AM, it is a threat
       as jobs will be automated and disappear.

       For yet other citizens, AI/AM-technology may be an opportunity to live a new life in a new human
         civilization of hybrids and truly artificial beings.

       In the poorest regions AI/AM-technology will not be an option, unless all SDG’s are achieved.

Targets and discussions

MindFuture wants to promote and provoke a debate of all the sustainable technology development, but in
particular with focus on the 18th Global Goal. We therefore created a separate platform for this purpose.
Recognizing the enormity of work to be done for reaching the 17 Global Goals, we believe that all the goals can
only be achieved if we harvest the potentials of next-generation artificial intelligence technology. The 18th Global
Goal – “Life with Artificials” – focuses upon how our future lives will be entangled with artificials, i.e. robotics,
autonomous agents, artificial intelligence and artificial mindsets that can control our economic systems, empower
political decision making, enhance our human minds and be our life companions. For this we need to educate
people to co-exist and work together with artificials with a set of common global rules.

             A GLOBAL CODE OF CONDUCT FOR ARTFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND ARTIFICIAL MINDSETS

               By 2030, all nations and international organizations must form a shared global code of conduct for
                  AI development of software and algorithms to be used in Artificials, and shall before implementing
                any self learning systems consider moral implications.


              STANDARDS FOR DEVELOPING ARTIFICIALS

                      All nations, companies, developers, and researchers are liable for any kind of Artificials projects,
               and be eligible to present functionality and reliability to ensure the that highest possible ethical
                 standards of practice are transparent.

                ARTIFICIALS MUST DECLARE THEMSELVES

                    Artificials shall be easily identifiable, and when in situations that may have a significant
               impact on consequences for endangering human lives, they must declare so and bee able to
                  explain why decisions are made the way they are.

                ARTIFICIALS IS FOR THE GOOD OF ALL HUMANITY

                    Artificials is developed and implemented on a notion of being a companion to all human
                  individuals to better communication and understanding of a common future civilization
               with respect of cultural differences.

              VIOLATION OF PRIVACY MUST BE PROHIBITED

                    Artificials must be constructed and implemented so that data acquired to perform tasks is
                protected to prevent use in unfair discrimination against both individuals and/or groups of
              any community.

             CORPORATIONS AND DEVELOPERS ARE ALWAYS ACCOUNTABLE FOR ARTIFICALS

            Humans must always take responsibility for artificials projects developing unintentional, or
                going wrong, and will be held accountable for damages done.
