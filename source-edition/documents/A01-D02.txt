Proposal THE MINDFUTURE BLOGWithin the charter of MFF’s vision statement PURPOSE: To build a forum to support debates of dilemmas, taboos and irrationality of decisions and actions, i.e. we’re addressing the sanity of the political agenda [sic].FORMAT: As we’re working and experimenting with AI/AM’s, we will have to do “better” than just deliver a column, or an article, to match traditional media outlets. We have our own website and want to communicate via this (preferable by use of mail/news to registered recipients), but will also announce “news” via classic press releases, (and we will of course be liable to press ethics and freedom of speech). The contemplations we’ve had are as follows:We’re not a traditional medium – but we do want to spur debates about [...]We want to provoke with our art-based approach – but actually we do have rather specific opinions on our own (e.g. climate, energy, etc.)Basically we want to build a “blog”. It doesn’t necessarily have a regular scheduled publishing sequence, and it could be coordinated with significant political [and other] events and/or orchestrated in combination with art-based events/exhibitions.Essentially we will produce (max) 1-2 pages with a specific topic, which is structured along a short executive abstracted postulate that unfolds with references to more information about the theme. The communicator will be in a “3rd person”-style, e.g. introducing the theme along the lines of: “… if I was an artificial mindset – a computer with access to all available data and able to analyze the rationality of […]” – this is where ARMI can address and talk about taboos and topics to start debates that ordinary people couldn’t initiate morally or ethically – or it could e.g. look like the enclosed example, which suggest to discuss the theme with our AM-chatbot (see also paragraph 6 below). Do we have to become a (niche) medium – or rather, will we be perceived as one?[…]Who do we want to talk to?People with social interest and strong responsibility; who wants to be informed and who seek a more rational approach to society’s problems and development – locally as well as the global 17 UN SDG’s. Who would followers [of MFF] want to talk to?People who are like-minded – but surely also politicians to feed debates and set the agenda, and provide new angles to solve our problems.How and where will this unfold?Debates starting the same way as one e.g. read the newspaper “Information” to look for inspiration to a key note subject, or e.g. become curious and catching up on Quora Digist (http://www.quora.com/). The Qoura-style is recommended – but for inspiration, do see this example too: https://woebot.io/, and do also take a quick look at his initiative about the making of a movement: https://hackernoon.com/how-350-phds-and-ai-researchers-are-banding-together-to-fight-the-corona-outbreak-7we532xi BRAND: Being MFF, we should be able to actually demonstrate various forms of different AM persona’s that will inspire to debate on our forum – and we will have to figure out how to combine and design a combination of Qoura and Woebot-styles, as well as defining a relevant dataset to run though ML.We could for instance name the 1-2 page-versions as a series of “The Artificial Mindset's view on humans” and e.g. brand it like:LEARNING FROM MACHINES; MIND THE BOTS; THE MINDFUTURE POSTULATES; THE ARTIFICIALS DILEMMAS; THE ARTIFICIAL CHRONICLES; THE AMIND AXIOMS, etc. (feel free to be creative; and by the way “amind” means: of a mind; to bear in mind).TOPICS: Obviously we could make a lot of issues into important topics and subject matters, but let’s limit to five and test first (feel free to sex-up the headlines too):  Politics, business, work and living: Behavior during the corona virusLiving in cities v rural: Before and after the pandemic Assessing energy consumption and transportation issuesPopulation growth: Longevity and migrations  Future food production, vertically and horizontal – see the enclosed example: POLITICS, BUSINESS, WORK AND LIVING; Rational and irrational behavior during the corona virus.
