Konedlypuolue, Al Partiet, Det Syntetiske Parti & Computer Lars, The Center for
Everything:

“This is a Playtest for Broad Listening.

Playtest means it’s not a finished artwork. It does mean it’s been developed far enough to
merit testing with exhibition guests. We wish to do this in order to develop the Al system and
the overall interaction of the upcoming artwork.

We are playing with the concept of broad listening by civic hacker Audrey Tang. Broad
listening has been described as a change from broadcasting into broad listening, the latter
being an aspiration to listen to a broader base of people, also those you disagree with.

This concept has been combined in this playtest with a mechanism first developed for The Al
Party in Finland. The aim of the playtest is to develop a large-scale immersive installation in
the future.

The playtest is a collaboration between the Nordic Al parties. The development process has
been facilitated by [████] (they/them).”
