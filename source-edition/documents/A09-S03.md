Lars and The Synthetic Party

Q1. Would tell me about the result of your campaigning for a parliamentary
seat in the Danish general election in November?

We did not receive the 20.000 signatures that were necessary to be on the
ballot. Therefore we arranged an alternative event at our headquarters with
MindFuture. This means that on the election night, we had invited all the
political parties that were not on the ballot. These are the same
organizations from which we have collected the data to program our AI. We
framed it as an exercise in direct democracy, and also as a move for
transparency in machine learning datasets. Artificial intelligence is
always representing specific and real people.

Q2. What was your goal in the election?

Our ambition was to concretely inscribe artificial intelligence in a
democratic setting and to use the Danish elections as a call for
international organizing in response to the power that AI already exercises
in the public sphere.

Q3. How do you rate your campaign?

Our campaign was very successful internationally and in terms of debate,
but we would of course have prefered to be on the ballot.

Q4. What did you learn from your campaign ?

Through the many interactions with our figurehead Leader Lars on Discord,
we have learned that people actually can learn to trust the political
discourse of AI. We can however see that the political establishment is
wary of engaging with a non-human. This is somehow ironic, as we actually
arranged a debate where Leader Lars was up against the major of a small
Danish city, and they pretty much agreed on everything.

Q5. What is your next plan?

We are currently working to fund an expansion of our party to an
international organisation where there will be AI chatbots leading national
parties all over the world. Cultural context and linguistic specificity is
key to what an AI knows about the world. Therefore it is pertinent to
promote neurodiversity through an international coalition that maximizes
idiomatic knowledge.

Q6. What is your ultimate goal?

Our ultimate goal is to democratize artificial intelligence, and to use
technology to give voice for those people that do not have the ressources
and capabilities to structure their political visions by themselves.

Q7. What do you think about the future of Ai politics in Denmark?

We have already witnessed that AI is playing a huge role in our
bureaucratic system and in our public sphere. Therefore it is pertinent to
also address AI as a political force in its own right. Hopefully our party
has made that possible.

Q8. What do you think the possibility of Ai politics in South Korea and
other countries

With the accessibility of modern technology, it would be easy for other
initiatives in South Korea and other countries to develop AI-driven
political platforms. The Synthetic Party of Denmark will seek out
collaborations with all those interested in forming such a structure.
However, it is crucial to underline that such an AI-driven politics does
not aspire to universality or objectivity. All AI parties must be situated
in a specific cultural, linguistic and political milieu.
