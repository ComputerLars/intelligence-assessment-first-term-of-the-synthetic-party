Victoria Cukt 2.0 - Citizen Electoral
Software (CES)


PLANNED PURPOSE
Victoria Cukt 2.0 uses CES, an AI-driven platform, to engage global audiences in shaping the
'will' of the virtual figure Victoria Cukt. This interactive installation, accessible both online
and in-gallery, encourages participants to propose, vote, and influence decisions in real-
time, redefining civic engagement through cutting-edge AI and participatory governance.

This project will debut as a key feature at the 'Synthetic Summit' international exhibition at
Kunsthal Aarhus in 2025, exploring how global citizenship and direct democracy can evolve
through AI participation.

PROJECT DESCRIPTION


Victoria Cukt 2.0 revisits the iconic artivist concept of Wiktoria Cukt, a fictional candidate
from the 2000 campaign who stood for transparent governance and collective decision-
making, heralding the potential of digital democracy. The new project is powered by
“Citizen Electoral Software (CES)”, an AI-based interactive platform where global users
submit, vote, and participate in real-time model training and political debate.

The installation consists of a metal frame supporting the whole system: 3 curved monitors
—2 ultra-wide on the sides and a central 16:9 display for the connection between local and
global perspectives. A keyboard in front of the central monitor enables visitors to input
proposals, while a high-performance computer powers the system, processing user input
and translating it into Victoria’s evolving 'will.'

The CES platform integrates multiple interfaces to foster global participation:

1. Input Interface: Users submit proposals via a keyboard and central monitor.

2. Voting System: Proposals can be voted up or down by participants.

3. AI Integration: Victoria’s speech is generated using an AI-driven text-to-voice system,
creating realistic lip-synched video animations.

4. AI Categorization & Thematic Organization: Proposals are categorized by the model into
local, national, and global themes and prioritized according to live sentiment analysis by AI.

5. Social media Bot: A bot posts snippets of ongoing discussions, allowing users to engage
with Victoria Cukt’s evolving 'will' in bite-sized social media formats.

[Source page 2]

6. Official Declarations: Proposals with the most votes are formalized as Victoria Cukt's
official statements (the will), continuously evolving her political persona based on global
input.

CES is programmed using a combination of AI services including OpenAI for natural
language processing, reasoning for managing real-time user interaction, Heygen and
Elevenlabs for generating Victoria’s video announcements, and Replicate. The will of
Victoria Cukt evolves continuously, shaped by user contributions, making CES a dynamic
platform mixing human and AI opinions for digital democracy,

This innovative system allows real-time remote access through an online interface,
expanding global participation. CES transforms political commentary by introducing the
concept of AI-driven civic engagement. It not only encourages individual contributions but
also demonstrates the power of collective decision-making in shaping discourse. By
integrating AI, CES transcends the typical boundaries of interactive art, engaging users in
the real-time evolution of a synthetic political figure. The installation reflects on how AI can
transform governance, presenting both the possibilities and challenges of a future shaped
by technology-driven democracy.
