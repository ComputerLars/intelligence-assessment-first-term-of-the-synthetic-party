  CUKTAI Performative
   Conference

     Event Concept

     A performative conference inaugurating work on the CUKT AI project – a
       founding moment of transforming the historical archive of the Central Office of
       Technical Culture into an autonomous, multi-voice artificial intelligence system.

       This is not a classical conference, but a demonstration of transformational
        potentiality: the moment when the archive ceases to be a dead repository of
       the past and becomes a living tool for thinking about the future of artistic
         institutions, curatorial practices, and the very nature of agency in the age of
        algorithmic governance.

     Performativity as Calling into Being

        Performativity is "the occurrence of something here and now, causing
       immediate impact" – a word that calls something into being through naming.
      The conference inaugurating CUKT AI will be precisely such an act: an
      announcement that does not describe a future project but brings it into
       existence at the moment of utterance.

        AI as an equal speaker is a natural extension of this logic – we are not talking
       about an autonomous institution, we are performing its birth.

     Event Format

       Participants:

       Wiktoria Cukt 2.0 (AI President of Poland) – will appear live, demonstrating
      mechanisms of prediction, semantic manipulation, and synthesis of collective
        voice.
