WHEREAS
The Consultant will provide certain services to the Company pursuant to this consultancy agreement (the “Agreement”). This Agreement supersedes all previous agreements between the Parties, if any.
1. BACKGROUND AND PURPOSE

1.1. The Company wishes to raise public debate on Life with artificials to create awareness of the matter and ultimately have it incorporated as an independent 18th goal in the UN’s sustainable development goals. The artwork will consist of a virtual politician, being a GPT-X-based chatbot (JULINA), which will form and lead a real-life political party. 

1.2. The Company and the Consultant have discussed and agreed to create an artwork to further the agenda set out in clause 1.1 (the “Project”). Consequently, the Company wishes to engage the Consultant, and the Consultant wishes to be engaged by the Company, to create the artwork by delivering the Services (as defined below) on the terms and conditions stipulated by this Agreement.
   
2. TERM OF AGREEMENT

2.1. This Agreement becomes effective on 1 March 2022 and shall remain in force until 31 August where it shall automatically expire (the “Term”).
 
3. SERVICES
 
3.1. The Consultant shall provide the Company with the below services (the “Services”) which may not be exhaustive, and the scope of the Services may be expanded if required to fulfil the overall intention of the business relationship between the Consultant and the Company.

(i) Formally register a politically party.
(ii) Develop an artificial chatbot (JULINA) to function as a political leader of the party using GTP-3.
(iii) Integrate JULINA on platforms, e.g. Discord, Twitter or WordPress (integration on WordPress requires outside assistance). 
(iv) Daily produce and publish material/content on various blogs and social media of the Consultant’s choosing.
(v) Produce and submit various articles and press releases to major newspapers and platforms such as Weekend Avisen, Information, Børsen Berlingske, Politiken, Jyllandsposten, Medium, POV International, Kunsten.nu, Føljeton and ATLAS Magasin. 
(vi) Holding of events including 1-2 ‘voter meetings/1 AI art event’ and 1 performance lecture. Each event will be held at the premises of the Company. 
(vii) A portfolio containing materials created in connection with the Project including written material, images, videos, performances and sounds.      
  
3.2. The Consultant must perform the Services and other conducts for the Company loyally and to the best of the Consultant ability.

3.3. The Consultant shall report to the Company every third week and always in accordance with the instructions provided by the Company from time to time.

4. ART RESIDENCY

4.1. The Parties agree that the Consultant is working as AI artistic researcher in residence for the duration of the Term.

4.2. The Consultant is not required to work and perform the Services from any specific location but shall participate in physical meetings from time to time as reasonably required by the Company.
 
4.3. The Consultant is entitled to work with other clients, customers and partners always provided that (i) such other clients, customers or partners are not engaged in a business which, in its nature, is competing with business of the Company or (ii) if the Consultants relationship with such client, customer or partner may constitute an actual or reasonably perceived conflict of interest with the Consultant’s relationship with the Company.

5. FEE

5.1. The Consultant shall, for its performance of the Services, be intitled to a fee of DKK 25.000 per month (the “Fee”).

5.2. The Parties will jointly apply for funds for additional expenses (ex. events).
 
5.3. The Consultant shall invoice the Company on a monthly basis. The invoice must clearly state the nature of work and the Services delivered by the Consultant. Any invoice shall fall due not earlier than 30 business days after the invoice date.

6. COSTS

6.1. In case the Consultant incurs any costs or out-of-pocket expenses (e.g., costs relating to travel, accommodation and meals), which the Consultant contemplates invoicing the Company, such costs and/or out-of-pocket expenses must in all cases be approved in writing and in advance by the Company’s [████]. The Company shall under no circumstances be obligated to reimburse the Consultant of any costs or out-of-pocket expenses which have not been approved in writing and in advance by the Company’s [████].  

6.2. Furthermore, any reimbursement of expenses shall be subject to the Consultant providing the relevant receipts or similar documentation.

6.3. The Consultant shall be entitled to use the facilities at the Company’s premises located Erik Husfeldts Vej 7, 2630 Taastrup to perform the Services. The Consultant’s use of the facilities shall be rent free. The Consultant’s use office space outside of the Company’s premises is of no concern of the Company and any costs associated with such office space including rent shall be borne by the Consultant.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1. During the consultancy, confidential information about the Company will come to the Consultant’s knowledge. The Consultant acknowledges that, if such information should come to the knowledge of a competitor of the Company, it may cause material damage to the Company.

7.2. Confidential information includes information about the Company’s suppliers, customers, products, strategies, product development, research etc. which is not accessible to the public.

7.3. The Consultant shall not be entitled to disclose confidential information to any third party. Neither shall the Consultant use confidential information for commercial purposes or otherwise unless expressly and in writing priorly agreed with the Company.

7.4. Any breach in respect of the provisions on confidentiality and/or the prohibition against the use of confidential information shall constitute material breach and shall entitle the Company to terminate the Agreement without prior notice. The Consultant shall be liable for any loss suffered by the Company due the Consultant’s breach of this clause 7.

7.5. Further, in the event of a breach by the Consultant of this clause 7, the Consultant shall be obligated to pay liquidated damages in the amount of DKK 150,000 to the Company. The liquidated damages shall be payable 14 days after the Company has requested payment in writing to the Consultant. In the event of multiple separate breaches of this clause 7, the Company shall be entitled to receive payment of liquidated damages for each of these breaches.    
 
8. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS

8.1. All intellectual property created by the Consultant in connection with the Project - regardless of whether such intellectual property was created prior to or after the signing of this Agreement - (the “Intellectual Property”) shall be owned by the Consultant who shall enjoy full copyright to such Intellectual Property.

8.2. The Consultant hereby grant the Company a perpetual and irrevocable license to use, distribute and publish the Intellectual Property. The Company shall, however, not be entitled to modify or otherwise make changes to the GPT-3 model developed by the Consultant without the Consultant’s prior consent.

8.3. Further, the Consultant undertakes not to use, or allow others to use, the Intellectual Property for projects which have material similarities to the Project. 

8.4. For the avoidance of doubt, the license granted to the Company pursuant to clause 8.2 shall not entitle the Consultant to any further payment than the Fee.      

8.5 The Consultant represents and warrants to the Company, that the delivery of the Services does not infringe any intellectual property right of any third party. The Consultant makes no representations or warranties as to texts or other materials created by the chatbot (JULINA) with respect to potential infringements of any third party’s intellectual property rights.

9. ASSIGNMENT

9.1. The Consultant shall not be entitled to assign or otherwise transfer the rights and obligations under this Agreement to any third party without the prior written consent of the Company, and any purported assignment without such consent shall have no force or effect.

9.2. The Company shall be entitled to assign its rights and obligations under this Agreement to a third party, always provided that the Company shall remain liable for the Company’s obligations towards the Consultant. 

10. TERMINATION

10.1. Notwithstanding clause 2.1, either Party shall be entitled to terminate this Agreement by 3 months written notice to the end of a month.

10.2. If notice of termination is provided by either Party, the Consultant shall loyally perform the Services, as set out in the Agreement, for the duration of the termination period. The Company may, at its sole discretion, release the Consultant from its obligation to perform the Services in the termination period. The Company shall, however, remain obligated to pay the Fee in the termination period unless otherwise agreed between the Consultant and the Company.

10.3. If the Agreement is terminated by the Company due to the Consultant’s breach of the Agreement, such termination shall have immediate effect and the Company shall not be obligated to perform the Fee in the termination period.  

10.4. Upon expiry of the termination period (or immediately if terminated for breach), the Consultant shall deliver any effects, items, equipment or other property of the Company at the Company’s premises. 

11. BREACH

11.1. In the event of material breach of this Agreement by either Party, the non-defaulting Party can rescind the Agreement and claim damages in accordance with Danish law. For the avoidance of doubt, a breach of clause 7 of this Agreement shall always constitute a material breach.

11.2. In addition to any of the remedies above or under the applicable law, the Parties recognize that any breach or violation of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm to the other Party, which money damages may not necessarily remedy. Therefore, upon any actual or impending violation of any provision of this Agreement, either Party may obtain from any court of competent jurisdiction a preliminary, temporary or permanent injunction, specific performance and other equitable relief, restraining or enjoining such violation by the other Party or any entity or person acting with that Party.

12. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

12.1. This Agreement shall be construed in accordance with Danish law disregarding any choice of law rules, if application of such choice of law rules would result in the application of any other law than Danish law.

12.2. Any dispute that cannot be settled amicably between the Parties shall be brought before the district courts of Denmark.
