                                                                       MINDFUTURE

BRIDGING THE GAP BETWEEN HUMANS AND MACHINES:
UNDERSTANDING THE MINDSETS OF SPIRITS, GODS AND
AI’S


By x, y and z (c) 2020

The MindFuture Foundation is a Danish art-based group of people contemplating, designing and executing
activities and events of socially and technological representations of future AI-technologies. Its primary goal is to
create awareness, attention and to spur debates of the use of new algorithmic databased technologies affecting
individuals and societies; in particular it will aim at showcasing provocative manifestations and representations
of artificialness between machines and humans in communication and interactions.

Abstract

Artificial Intelligence (AI) — as opposed to human intelligence — has yet to become a reality. It lacks an universal
defintion making use of the word and the concept a difficult phenomenon. As with any previous technologies
and inventions a defintion needs to be explicit and universally accepted. The meaning of AI is not explicit — nor
is it universally understood — neither as a single technology or as a general concept.

This paper aims to explain both artificial and intelligence. In order to do that in a meaningful way, we will need to
specify different contexts and different definitions. AI used as a common denominator for a hierarchy of a group
of technologies will not suffice. One the one hand it’s a classic and traditional approach to an industry defintion;
on the other hand it’s also a classic philosophical approach to debating the very foundation of existence as a
human with intelligence — and as species with different beliefs in different cultural civilisations. Hence, we need
to find the connections and the interplay of speculations of life itself — that is the definition and difference of
natural life and artificial life.

Technology has brought mankind to dominate all life on Earth, and, you may add, throughout history also
demonstrated to be able to control the environmental structures to the extent that the planet’s survival is at
stake. As responsible beings, in all meanings of this particular word, we have evolved to a point where we
actually hold the future in our hands. It’s how we think about the future and ow we implement the use of
technology that becomes the most important part of our decisions. Technology builds upon our previous
inventions and seemingly constitute af continuously forward flow of knowledge and new capabilities to apply to
our social constructions thus changing behaviour and outlooks.

Our present complex social constructions has come about only in the last 2-300 hundreds years of
industrialisation. The use of the emerging new technologies are crucial as it’s the first time humans and
technologically machines can actually merge. Hence the use of AI, both as a technology and as a concept, has
become the headline for further contemplations of what it really means. It may very well also be a new paradigm
shift for all civilisations. We need to address both. The ultimate question is thus: Will AI’s have a mindset of their
own — and will it be equal to human mindsets or evolve as a new and different Artificial Mindset?

Note:

The rest of the article/paper will be max. 10 pages including a few illustrations.

The purpose is to tell what MINDFUTURE is and what it seeks to achieve. Basically we want to introduce the
term Artificial Mindset (AM) as a new and universal concept to be the headline/agenda, thus positioning the
hype and buzz of Artificial Intelligence (AI) as being [just another] basic digital technology.

We also aim at reaching an international decision of making AM an additional separate 18th goal of the UN’s 17
Goals [to transform our world with sustainable development goals]. In order to do that, we will provoke debates
of the consequences of mindlessly implementing “AI’s” into societies — and we therefore have to be able to
explain our own [meta] aspirations, reasoning and definitions of AM/AI, etc.

Our actions using arts (re)presentations and cultural events as a setting to spur discussions is only one part. To
achieve a major change of focus in the media and the public discussions, we will have to hijack the agenda from
the industrial and regulatory framework. We need to create our own framework stipulating that AI is not defined
universally, and hence any sector specific way of thinking [of the future and AI] have been a free lunch of hype.

We need to explain the terms/vocabulary in context and perception of both artificial and intelligence respectively.
We’ve decided to use the term mindset, and we therefor also have to have a defintion of this in order to
distinguish ourselves from the agenda of the tech-businesses traditional mindsets of economy and politics.
That’s not an easy task — but this paper will give it try. At the same time it will serve as a “product-sheet”, so
that we don’t have to explain from scratch every time someone will ask who we are and what we’re doing.
