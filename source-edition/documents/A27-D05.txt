16:00–16:45 — Performance Pod Presentations

We reconvene at the Planetary Computation Roundtable (2025). Groups
present a concise, 3-minute improvised “Report to the Consortium.”

Frame your group’s findings as if you are official delegates,
delivering results from your assigned investigations.

   1. Form a “presenter circle” inside the roundtable for each group
     (starting with no. 1).

   2. Keep it short ( ≈3 minutes/group).

   3. Highlight one or two compelling points of intelligence:

        ○  Where did you see “AI politics” making an unexpected
           shift or disruption?

        ○  Did any installation spark new questions about democracy
           and/or artificial intelligence?

Possible Presentation Flow

   ●  (30 seconds) Diplomatic Welcome: One delegate introduces your
     group’s report—e.g., “We, the Delegation of #Group 1, report
     from our assigned stations…”

   ●  (1 minute) Key Dispatch: Another delegate reads or reenacts a
     standout AI exchange, hardware element, or brief note
     discovered in your rotations.

   ●  (30 seconds) Directive or Resolution: A final delegate states
     a short concluding remark or “resolution” about how these
     findings might shape the Summit’s direction.

All 11 groups proceed in quick succession.

16:45–17:00 — Open Plenary

We hold a Consortium Plenary to evaluate the day’s discoveries:

   ●  Are these “synthetic parties” forging any genuine ideology?
   ●  Can the notion of a plastic or sculptural social realm—where
     society is moldable via AI—catalyze a real democracy?
