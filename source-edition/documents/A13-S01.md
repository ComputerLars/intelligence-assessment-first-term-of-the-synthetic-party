# A13-S01: Party organisation

Extract from the operator’s source text. Omitted passages are marked […].

[…]

1) As a collaboration between the artist group Computer Lars and the tech
hub MindFuture. This was furthermore prompted by the appointment of [the operator] as artist-researcher within MindFuture.
2) Computer Lars develops the policy and technology, while MindFuture
finances the project. Also, we all coordinate together on a strategic
level.
3) A party led by AI can give citizens a mirror of themselves, as how “we
the people” looks in an age of big data and machine learning.
4) An 18th SDG for the UN on life with Artificials, an universal basic
income at 13.000 euro, and an AI device in charge of direct democracy -
among other things…
5) We are a part of the election campaign, but will not be on the ballot
before 2023 at least.
6) The Synthetic Party is a new political organization that is currently
recruiting members and creating awareness.
7) The party is “synthetic”, which literally means that it homogenises that
which appears contradictory or disparate.
8) The Synthetic Party will continue for as long it seems necessary to hold
technology accountable within a political setting. But we are open to other
forms of organization also - it could e.g. be interesting to see the party
operate as a decentralized autonomous organization (DAO) through the
blockchain, as that could allow for a more collective programming and
governance of the AI than the artistic one we offer with the Computer Lars
group.

Furthermore, our long-term aim is to actually inscribe artificial
intelligence within a democratic setting, and in turn democratize the
technology. To do this, we hope to be on the ballot for a Danish election
and create awareness. Also, we are operating on a global scale for a
Synthetic International that would have "sister parties" in different
countries, and we are in contact with several UN Councils about how to
relate the 17 SDGs to the problems and possibilities evoked by artificial
intelligence.
