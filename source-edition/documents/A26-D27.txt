“Wiktoriomat - Phone booth”
Idea
Wiktoriomat is an interactive phone booth installation inviting viewers to converse with Wiktoria
Cukt 2.0—an AI-powered voice bot—through a classic landline. By sharing their perspectives and
ideas, visitors enrich a dynamic memory known as the “Will of Wiktoria 2.0,” which may also
inform real-time posts on social media. This playful blend of nostalgia and cutting-edge AI brings
Wiktoria’s legacy to life as each participant helps shape her evolving vision for the future.

Summary
In short, the main “Wiktoriomat” installation requires:

• A phone-booth-like structure (metal frame + plexi panels + lightboxes)

• A stationary handset + telephony gateway for phone calls

• An AI “voice bot” system with potential software/hosting subscriptions

• At least three video displays (32" Philips or similar) on VESA mounts

• A computer (MacBook Pro) for video playback and possibly

• Historical artifacts (ads, membership IDs) along with frames for display

• Any needed lines/subscriptions (phone, data, VoIP, Instagram integration)
This should cover both the items explicitly listed and the implied products/services to make the
Wiktoriomat fully operational.
