Unfortunately, your application has been rejected

Dear [████],

Danish Arts Foundation’s Committee for Visual Art Project Funding has processed your application to
the pool Production and Exhibition and made a decision about a grant for your project: [████].

Unfortunately, we have to inform you that the committee has decided to reject your application.

Why has your application been rejected?

The Committee has not made a decision on your application, because you either did not send in or fill
out a motivated application text/mandatory attachments/mandatory sound-, picture- or movie-files, or
because other mandatory attachments are lacking. Your application has therefore been rejected. Your
application lacked:

       There must be at least 4 pages of visual material and a clearly marked sketch for the newly
       produced work.

The project can be reapplied for, if the starting date is after the next application deadline.

The committee received 397 applications

Among these, 207 projects have received grants for a total of DKK 6,586,661.

If you want to know how much funding other applicants have received, please take a look at our
website (in Danish).

Danish Arts Foundation’s Committee for Visual Arts Project Funding makes the final decision
concerning the distribution of the committee’s funds. This means that you are not able to make a
complaint about the decision to a higher administrative authority.
