THE Al PARTY is both a large-scale performarite
and the start of a new political movement. The
realisation at Mad House in 2018 was the official
constitutive meeting of the Finnish Al Party. It
consisted of The Al Party Headquarters, The
Constitutive Meeting of The Al Party and The Al
Party Party.

Working group: Sami Henrik Haapala, Kaisa
Kemikoski, Paula Lehtonen and Aku Meriläinen.
From an original concept by Bombina Bombast
(SWE), Center for Everything (FIN), First Kiss
Productions (NOR) and Triage Live Art Collective
(AUS/GER).
