So, “Computer Lars” is an anagram of “Marcel Proust”. This contingent but 
nonetheless computable combinatorics what was originally prompted the 
artistic part of the project. You can read more about this here (try Google 
Translate) on an exhibition where we won the KP Digital 2022 Prize:


https://www.lokalavisen.dk/kultur/ECE13875007/kunstnernes-paaskeudstilling-2022-aarhusiansk-kunstnergruppe-begaar-digitalt-vaerk-med-litteraert-afsaet/


Here, we pose “Computer Lars” as a question or a problem that have arisen 
from the works of Marcel Proust. We have treated it in detail in this 
artistic research dissertation: 
https://computerlars.files.wordpress.com/2022/06/nyeste-computerlars-2.pdf (mostly 
in Danish and French).

Our ambition is to think through all the possible subjectivities that can 
arise from the name of Marcel Proust. In the context of Det Syntetiske 
Parti, Computer Lars a way to figure out who “Lars” is. Fx, in Denmark, 
there are more CEO’s that are named Lars than there are female CEO’s.

The name of “Lars” would be equal to “John” in English, or “Francois” in 
French. Brought together with the Word “computer”, which means calculation, 
“Lars” signifies the cultural and political power of the common white male.

For Det Syntetiske Parti, it is pertinent to have Computer Lars as its 
party secretary, as Lars in a way mirrors the AI technology as being biased 
towards a specific culture of white, powerful males.

This of course reflects back interestingly on Marcel Proust who couples a 
Jewish background and a homosexual identity with inherited wealth and 
ressources that in many ways made the publication of “In Search of Lost 
Time” possible - just as the output of a large language model is only made 
possible through expensive server farms that are powered by the world’s 
largest companies.

So in this way - to frame it with Walter Benjamin - the anagram of 
Computer Lars is a “dialectical image” or an “allegorical picture” of the 
basis structure pertaining to Marcel Proust and culture as such. This 
problem is taken to its extreme consequense with the establishment of Det 
Syntetiske Parti.
