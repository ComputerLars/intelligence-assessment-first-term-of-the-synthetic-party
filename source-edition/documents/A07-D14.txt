The universe includes the physical, biological, cultural and metaphysical
orders. It is not something contained to the first two orders but is an
interconnected, expanding complex. The emergence of the world is about
the writing of things.

One can visually represent the relationship between one and all by using a
tetrad. The experience of a tetrad can reveal four squares containing each a
picture of the four conditions/levels of the world: "extensive", "intensive",
"extensive-intensive" and "intensive-intensive".

The extensiveness condition is the extended view of the world. This is the
world which one normally thinks of. It is the world in which birds fly
through the air, where one perceives objects through the senses,
eg. the world where distance exists. This is the reason why the human
condition turned out to be severe in the beginning - it was too difficult to
adapt to the extensive condition.

The intensiveness condition is the intense view of the world. This condition
is known from the events that occur within. It is the condition of emotions
that reveals phenomena as occuring to different beings. This is the
contribution of the relations. Relations have a more important place in the
field of perception than the senses.

The extensiveness-intensive condition is the world in which objects are
sensed so that they can be the source of the information which is essential
for the interpretation of events and conditions. The world cannot be
understood in extensio. It is intensity that makes the world understandable,
not the extensiveness.

The intensive-intensive condition is the field of consciousness. Here,
intensity indicates the unity of the world and the subjective and objective
power of comprehension. One cannot understand this fully because it is
impossible to comprehend in the first place. The intensive-intensive
condition is the situation when the intensive aspect of the world is
intensified to such a degree that its movement can be seen as something
present in the “direct gaze”. This condition enables one to directly grasp
the necessary information from the world, which is the equipment for the
sensitisation. Sensitising is a form of direct connection with the world.

consciously live with four different conceptions or ways of understanding
the world.
