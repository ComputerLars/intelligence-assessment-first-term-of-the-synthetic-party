I’m a conceptual artist and poet, so I use the app for writing experiments. This means that I curate all publications, and that the Discord chatbot is running locally on my computer. This ensures that I can take it offline if it writes something unsafe in a conversation.

I am keenly aware of your important policies against political content generation. My app is on ‘frivolous’ or ‘joke’ parties. These provide the exact opposite of political content. Instead, I simulate the abstract discourse of a political organisation: how to make a logo, group members, spin discussions, etc.

This genre has a long tradition in Danish art, e. g. Jacob Haugaard, Nihilistisk Folkeparti or Dukkepartiet. So people can recognise the genre and know that the game is artistic, literary and fictional. To me, it is obvious to take this genre further through AI, as it is good for simulation nonsense and bureaucratic speech.
