Authentication

Replace with your token:

NLPCloudClient = require('nlpcloud');

client = new NLPCloudClient('<model>','[████]'

Add your API token after the Token keyword in an Autho
rization header. You should include this header in all
your requests: Authorization: [████] <token>.
Alternatively you can also use Bearer instead of Token:

Authorization: [████] <token>.

Here is an example using Postman (Postman is
automatically adding headers to the requests. You should
at least keep the Host header, otherwise you will geta 4
00 error.):

Authorization
