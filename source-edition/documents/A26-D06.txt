DESCRIPTION OF THE PROJECT

The collaboration consists of research and development as well as presenting it as part
of a group exhibition ‘Synthetic Summit’ in Kunsthal Aarhus.

Asker Bryld Staunæs is the project leader of the exhibition project. In the working group
for the new artwork he is the visual artist and the AI developer.

[████]

The artwork has a working title of ‘The Global AI Party HQ’.

The artwork is an audiovisual and performative installation where the audience can
communicate with an AI about the future of politics. The AI will show what is the new
world like based on their discussion – not only presenting the ideas, but also drawing
data from the manner the discussion unfolded. The results will be presented as a 360
degrees audiovisual world surrounding the audience.

The artwork is first realized in Kunsthal Aarhus in the exhibition ‘Synthetic Summit’, but
will also be expanded later with partners from Finland, Germany, Sweden and further
partners from Denmark.
