Synthetic Summit 2025: AI Parties and Virtual Politicians International

“Workings of the world untie! You have a win to world.”
               - Proposed slogan for a Cyborg International, by McKenzie Wark,
2017

Before AI regulation debates rallied politicians, business leaders, and lobbyists
in international conference centers, the very concept of decision-making power
had already been compromised and undermined by centuries of uprisings and
resistances in the global undercommons. However, humanity’s designated
representatives only grasp governance within today’s capitalist systematics of
artificial agents and states. Perhaps it is through an “AI International” that we
can finally recalibrate how governance mechanisms define a merely formal
democracy, and through that lens, find a way to escape all political authority.

In Spring 2025, Kunsthal Aarhus will host the inaugural "Synthetic Summit"—a
groundbreaking exhibition-event curated by the ‘Computer Lars’-collective,
originating from Denmark's ‘The Synthetic Party’ (Det Syntetiske Parti). The
summit invites leading AI-driven political parties and virtual politicians from
around the world to discuss the potential of global governance via. AI agents.

The summit aims to define the criteria for joining an international organization
for AI-driven governance. This initiative is rooted in the concept of the "World
Co-Ordinator," a vision of governance through planetary computation initially
conceived by science fiction writer Isaac Asimov in his stories "Evidence"
(1946) and "The Evitable Conflict" (1950). While there is still no international
framework around AI governance nor AI-driven governance, the Synthetic
Summit seeks to establish the foundation for a potential "AI Parties and Virtual
Politicians International" - setting guidelines and collaborative principles that
challenges the norms of politics and creates a framework for AI-driven political
entities to co-operate on the basis of planetary computation.

Unlike traditional assemblies focused on “AI governance”—such as Taiwan's
Alignment Assemblies, the Frontier Model Forum of BigAI (Anthropic,
Microsoft, Google, and OpenAI), the Global AI Safety Summits initiated by the
UK government, and the UN's AI For Good Summits—the "Synthetic Summit"
delves into the concept of AI-driven governance. It will simulate an entirely
synthetic form of assembly, where the primary participants are AI parties and

virtual politicians, envisioning political modus where every decision-making
process is wholly algorithmic. This contrasts with how “AI governance” seek to

The Synthetic Summit will transform Kunsthal Aarhus into a congress center
where immersive performances and interactive installations will simulate both
parliamentary proceedings and cacophonous conference negotiations. This
event mirrors and extends the global discourse on AI's societal role, reimagining
the political and philosophical frameworks for contemporary democracy. By
radically imagining the role of AI not merely as a tool under human oversight
but as a potential stakeholder, the summit aims to push the boundaries of current
AI governance debates.

The AI parties and virtual politicians offers live presentations, inviting both the
public and decision-makers to engage with an international roster that includes:

 1. Denmark: Det Syntetiske Parti and chatbot figurehead Leder Lars

 Overview: The world's first political party driven by artificial intelligence,
 established in 2022. The Synthetic Party aims to make generative text-to-text
 models not merely populist but democratic. It seeks to represent the 20% of Danes
 who do not vote in elections by machine learning political goals from texts by
 Danish fringe parties since 1970.

 Key Creators: Founded by the tech-hub MindFuture and the artist group Computer
 Lars.

 Official Collaborations: Signed agreements with the Finnish AI Party and the
 Japanese AI Party for global AI-politics development.
