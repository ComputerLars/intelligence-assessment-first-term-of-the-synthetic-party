THE AI PARTIES INTERNATIONAL

[████]

The AI Parties International:
The AI Parties International signifies a global assembly of AI-driven political parties and virtual
politicians, with a goal of upending the paradigm of political democracy. The cornerstone of this
initiative is the mission of The Global AI Party, a potential UN entity envisioned to serve as an
"Earth Overseer." The Global AI Party, leveraging AI technologies and machine learning, would
undertake tasks of global decision-making, conflict resolution, policy-making, and enforcing eco-
friendly regulations.

The project aims to bring together stakeholders interested in the potential of AI. It’s fired by the
poverty of imagination which dominates our current political world order. The provocation is to
co-create with real AI political movements, and to present opportunities for dialogue within an
immersive installation as a response to a planet in crisis.

The Audience Experience:
Central to the performance are the headquarters of The Global AI Party. Designed as a multi-
sensorial and experiential installation that expands beyond logocentric concepts of AI and into

[Source page 2]

an embodied intelligence (not only human) which reflects the real entangled relationships of
the world. Audiences will be invited to explore the potential world/s of AI governance and to
participate in a curated program of activities, including – meetings, reading groups, debates,
panel discussions, interactive art works, interviews with special guests, AI experts, parties and
performances. Participants will have agency in terms of defining the structure and agenda of
The AI Parties International in their locale. The project will engage with real-life elections, f.e. an
AI mayoral campaign in Tokyo, and will invite guests from diverse communities - culminating in a
final voting session and rave with guest DJ’s and performers. The work will be the result of a
cumulative worldbuilding process– into which artists, scientists, hackers, AI politicians, ethicists,
and the public are invited to shape, and participate in the events.

Our venture extends beyond the familiar logocentric approach to AI. We look forward to
exploring multisensory immersive performances and the embodiment central to performing
arts. The focus is not only on the high-tech intricacies of AI, but on bringing forth novel and
unconventional forms of artistic and political representation – putting to the forefront queer
and BIPOC voices and those from the Global South.
