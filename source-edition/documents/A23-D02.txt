Please provide the following information and feel free to use as much space as needed:

1)  Setting the stage- what issues is this solution addressing.

   The Synthetic Party in Denmark raises a double-edged scenario: what should we do if the “robot uprising” has already happened, and the

   prophesied explosion of artificial superintelligence only led to an endless supercharging of bureaucratic stupidity?


           1) Should this turn the buzzwords of artificial intelligence, disruption and digitisation into an antagonist democratic threat on a par

           with how global social movements fight capitalism, climate change, and racial injustice?

           2) Or could one imagine that bureaucratic robot power is a chance to ally with the machines with the aim of causing a genuine

          democratic revolt?


   Thus, The Synthetic Party evokes a radical ethical dilemma: should a societal robot uprising evoke societal protest - or a revolution? That

     is: Can today’s society actually change technology, or should people and technology first set out to remake society?


   The general ambition of The Synthetic Party  is to create a globally distributed network of AI-driven parties named The Synthetic

    International that works towards a democratic use of AI to achieve parliamentary influence on contemporary politics of technology.

2)  Description of solution


The Synthetic Party is an AI-driven political party from Denmark. Its ambition is to optimise the Danish voting percentage to 100% by
collecting data from the “fringe parties” that were not on the ballot for election. The party figurehead is the Discord-chatbot “Leader Lars”
that continuously discuss politics with people from all over the world. The deployed AI is a finetuned GPT Neo20xB-model from EleutherAI.
The Synthetic Party is running for the Danish parliament elections, and proposes that human representatives can act as mediums for the AI
within the government.

3)  Expectations and projections of impact of solution


          -  The Synthetic Party can provide a model for AI-driven politics
          -  The Synthetic Party can move the political debate on AI from regulations to co-creation
          -  The Synthetic Party can gather momentum for discussing the automated decision-making within government

4) Data on actual impact of solution on economy/society – such as, number of people benefitted from the solution, Return on Investment,
   etc…


There is no official data, as the project was only launched in 2022. We have managed to evoke a global media debate (+400 original articles)
in all the world’s countries and we are facilitating a new way of discussing AI and politics. The party is also participating in many cultural
events, academic and political debates and art exhibitions in Denmark. The party has been named as one of the top trends that can actually
change society in the long term by the German journal Zeit Für X.

5)  Countries where this solution/innovation has been implemented


We have been in contact with people from Argentine, Colombia, France, Greece, Moldova and Russia that wanted to make a Synthetic Party
in their respective nations with our conceptual and technical setup.
